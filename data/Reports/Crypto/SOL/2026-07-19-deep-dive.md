The fundamental case is real but narrower than the headline numbers suggest. The regime and location gates do the work here: they suspend action without requiring a verdict on the fee-durability question the workers disagree on.

## Where the Workers Disagree

The crux: the quant worker's "SOL is 9x cheaper than ETH on a price/fees basis, with 1.8x ETH's absolute 30-day fee total" reads as a standing value gap — real, cited (own MCP DefiLlama + copytradeinsider), and persistent over a year, which argues SOL is mispriced cheap relative to its actual usage. The bear worker's read of the same underlying activity — memecoin share of DEX volume collapsing from 67.5% (late 2024) to 10-12% (mid-2026), fees down 84%, Pump.fun fees down 83% day-over-day, Q1 app revenue down 68% YoY, developer base down 30% — argues the "fee leader" framing is a mania artifact that is now unwinding, i.e., the numerator (fees) is procyclical and shrinking, so the cheap multiple is a value trap, not an opportunity, because it is a moving target getting worse, not a stable mispricing.

Both workers are citing largely the same underlying data (fee totals, DEX volume, Pump.fun metrics) and disagreeing on trend direction and durability, not on the facts themselves — this is a genuine, resolvable disagreement rather than two workers talking past each other. I weight the bear's trend read more heavily for the immediate call: an 84% fee decline and an 80% collapse in a top revenue driver (Pump.fun) inside a matter of months is a fast-moving deterioration that a "cheap on trailing fees" argument does not survive, because trailing multiples lag a collapsing numerator. But I do not go to Avoid, because the composition shift argument cuts both ways — volume "migrating" to Hyperliquid rather than "disappearing" means Solana's underlying settlement/stablecoin layer (the $15.4B stablecoin float, the $64.8B cumulative USDC mint) is not obviously impaired, only the memecoin-speculation layer that sat on top of it. That is a real distinction the bear worker's framing underweights.

That doubt does not survive the deterministic layer — the 200-MA was independently recomputed on OKX klines at $90.13, matching the Yahoo-sourced $90.08 to within 0.06%.
